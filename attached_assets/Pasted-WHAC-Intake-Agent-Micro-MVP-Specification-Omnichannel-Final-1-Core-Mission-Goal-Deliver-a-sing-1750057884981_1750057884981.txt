WHAC Intake Agent — Micro-MVP Specification (Omnichannel Final)
1. Core Mission
Goal:
Deliver a single, structured intake system that auto-captures every customer request—via SMS, email, or phone/voice—organizes all details, and ensures no job is lost or duplicated, no matter how the customer contacts WHAC.
2. Who & Why
- Primary User: Nikki (Office Manager)
- Backup Users: Dave (Owner), Step-Mom (Overflow)
- Pain: Manual entry and multi-channel chaos (texts, emails, phone) = lost jobs, double work, customer frustration
- Success: After 2 weeks, no more “lost” jobs, no more Excel re-entry, all intake routes visible in one place.
3. Supported Intake Channels & Routing
A. SMS Intake (Twilio)
- Customer texts the dedicated WHAC Twilio number.
- Message is captured via Twilio webhook and appears instantly as a “New Job” in the Intake Agent.
- Key fields auto-filled: Contact (SMS sender), Problem/Details (SMS body), Time/Source (auto-captured).
- Office manager reviews, adds missing info, and saves job.
B. Email Intake (SendGrid)
- Customer emails jobs@whac.com (or similar).
- Email parsed via SendGrid and appears as a “New Job” in the Intake Agent.
- Key fields auto-filled: Contact (from sender email), Problem/Details (email subject/body), Time/Source (auto-captured).
- Office manager reviews, adds/fixes details, and saves job.
Figure 1. SMS & Email Intake (Twilio/Sendgrid) flow

C. Voice Intake (Twilio Voice, with Overflow + Transcription + IVR)
- Customer calls the WHAC number (same as SMS).
- Twilio receives the call and routes as follows:
    - If Nikki (office manager) is available: Call is forwarded to Nikki’s phone/desk.
    - If Nikki does not answer or is busy: Call automatically forwards to Dave, then Step-Mom (overflow order).
- IVR answers if no live answer:
    - “Thank you for calling WHAC. If you are requesting service, press 1. To leave a message for billing or general inquiries, press 2.”
    - Press 1: Customer prompted to leave name, address, callback number, and description. Call is recorded and transcribed. Option to press 9 to request immediate callback.
    - Press 2: Message routed to billing voicemail.
- All calls are recorded and transcribed (Twilio service).
- New “Voice Job” appears in Intake Agent: Contact (caller ID), Problem/Details (transcript), Time/Source (auto), Transcript/Recording (links).
- Office manager reviews transcript, completes required fields (e.g., name/address), and saves job.

7. Acceptance Criteria
- All SMS, email, and voice calls appear as new jobs within 1 minute of receipt.
- Office manager can edit/complete required fields for any job.
- Transcripts and recordings for voice jobs are attached and reviewable.
- All jobs have source/received time auto-captured.
- Office manager can export all job data to CSV at any time.
- Callback requests are flagged and prioritized.
- After two weeks, Excel/Calendar is only used for legacy data, not new intake.
8. Out of Scope for MVP
- No multi-layer IVR or complex routing trees.
- No automated tech assignment.
- No analytics/dashboards.
- No inventory, quoting, or CRM integration.
- No automated scheduling of callback time (all callbacks are ASAP/manual).
9. Notes & Rationale
- “Bloat-free”: All automations only assist; no jobs get created/assigned without office manager review.
- Overflow routing for voice means no lost calls, and real coverage during peak/absence.
- Transcription is included, but if transcript is unclear, recording is always available.
- IVR is operator-first: efficient, not annoying, with structured service/billing split and callback option.
- This platform can demo as “true omnichannel FSM intake” to any future customer.
